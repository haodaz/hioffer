## Research experience
- **Undergraduate researcher, Pervasive HCI Group, Tsinghua** (Sep 2025 – present), advised by Prof. Li Wei.
  Ran a 14-participant interview and diary study with blind screen-reader users on how they verify LLM answers;
  designed "SourceSpeak", a screen-reader plug-in that reads provenance cues aloud. In a within-subjects study
  (n = 12), participants caught 2.3x more incorrect answers with the plug-in. Paper under review at a major
  HCI conference (first author).
- **Research intern, Microsoft Research Asia, Beijing** (Jun – Aug 2026), mentor: Dr. Zhang Hua.
  Conversational-agent study with 20 older adults (65–82) and their adult children; found that error repair
  initiated by a family member doubled continued use over four weeks. Short paper accepted at a workshop.
- **Course project, Human-Centered AI (Spring 2026):** a constraint-based design tool that flags
  inaccessible colour contrast and focus order in Figma prototypes; used by 3 student teams.
