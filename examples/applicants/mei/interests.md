# Research interests

Human-computer interaction at the intersection of accessibility and large language models:

1. Screen-reader users and LLM interfaces: how blind and low-vision users verify what a model says, and
   interface designs that expose provenance (where an answer came from) non-visually.
2. Older adults and conversational agents in Chinese households: trust, repair after errors, and family
   mediation of technology use.
3. Mixed-methods evaluation: interview studies and diary studies combined with instrumented prototypes.

Secondary: design tools that let non-programmers specify accessible UI constraints; participatory design.
