# Research interests

I want to work on robot learning for manipulation in unstructured homes, specifically:

1. Learning from few demonstrations: diffusion policies and visuomotor imitation that generalise across object
   instances and lighting, with a focus on cheap hardware (sub-$2,000 arms).
2. Tactile sensing for deformable objects (cloth, food), combining vision with low-cost tactile skins.
3. Sim-to-real for household tasks: domain randomisation and real-to-sim scene reconstruction so that policies
   trained in simulation transfer to a real kitchen.

Secondary: assistive robotics for elderly care in Indian households; safety constraints for learned policies.
