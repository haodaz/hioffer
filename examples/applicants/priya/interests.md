# Research interests

I want to work on natural language processing for low-resource and multilingual settings, especially:

1. Efficient adaptation of large language models to Indian languages (Tamil, Hindi, Kannada) with small amounts of data: parameter-efficient fine-tuning, tokenizer adaptation, and data selection.
2. Evaluation that actually measures what matters for multilingual users: cultural grounding, code-switching, and faithfulness, rather than translated English benchmarks.
3. Speech-text models for languages with mostly spoken (not written) data.

Secondary interests: retrieval-augmented generation for government and health information in Indian languages; model compression for on-device inference.
